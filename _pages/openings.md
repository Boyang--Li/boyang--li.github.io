# Open Positions

We currently don't have funding for **international** PhD students. We can only welcome you if you have **external funding** or through **exchange program**.

We are always looking for motivated and talented **domestic** students to join our Higher Degree by Research (HDR) program!

Research Areas Include:
<ul>
    <li> Aerial Robotics and Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) Systems</li>
    <li>Autonomous Navigation and Control </li>
    <li>Sensor Integration and Data Fusion</li>
    <li>Artificial Intelligence for Autonomous Systems</li>
    <li>Advanced Air Mobility (AAM) and eVTOL Aircraft Technology </li>
</ul>

Contact Information:

Please note the scholarship is only available for domestic student under the [Australian Government's Research Training Program (RTP)](https://www.newcastle.edu.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0011/942338/Guidelines-Strategic-Engagement-Scheme.pdf). 

For inquiries or further information, please contact Dr Boyang Li by Email: <boyang.li@newcastle.edu.au>.
